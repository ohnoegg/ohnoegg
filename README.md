## About Me

> **Understand the problem → Break it down → Learn what is needed → Build and improve.**

I'm a software engineer who enjoys turning ambiguous problems into executable plans and working products. I define the essential problem, split it into manageable tasks, learn the technologies required, and iterate until the solution works.

During **nine months as a startup co-founder (Jan–Sep 2026)**, I worked across software development and operations in a fast-changing environment. That experience shaped how I prioritize **business value**, make practical technical decisions, and use limited engineering resources effectively.

I also pursue independent software products through **DevIT**. My current interests are **AI agents, multimodal search, and end-to-end product engineering**—especially the intersection of model capabilities and reliable user-facing systems.
